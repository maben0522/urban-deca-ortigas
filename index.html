Questions buyers ask

Is it ready for occupancy?

Ready-for-occupancy options are available based on current inventory. Confirm exact availability before reservation.

What unit sizes are available?

2BR B at 30.60 sqm, 2BR A and 2BR C at 35.57 sqm, and 3BR A at 42.07 sqm.

Can I use Pag-IBIG financing?

Pag-IBIG options may be available, subject to unit eligibility, buyer qualification, approval and requirements.

How much is the monthly amortization?

It depends on the unit, financing type, loan amount, term, rate and the latest approved computation.

How much is the downpayment?

It differs by unit, building and current promotion. Request the latest computation.

Can I schedule a site viewing?

Yes. Message MAB with your preferred date and time.

How does the referral program work?

Qualified partners may receive ₱25,000 per successful transaction, released once the referred buyer is approved, subject to validation and program terms.
