Project Background

About Urban Deca Homes Ortigas

Urban Deca Homes Ortigas is a large master-planned residential community along Ortigas Avenue Extension in Rosario, Pasig City.

The official project information describes a development covering approximately 13 hectares and composed of 22 residential towers, built around a central community park and amenity area.

8990 Holdings' corporate disclosures identify Urban Deca Homes Ortigas as a project designed to serve first-time homebuyers in Pasig and nearby cities, offering 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom homes for different household needs.

Depending on current inventory, buyers may encounter ready-for-occupancy or other available inventory. Always request the latest unit availability and computation before deciding.

FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask

What is Urban Deca Homes Ortigas?

It is a large master-planned residential community along Ortigas Avenue Extension in Rosario, Pasig City, developed under the Urban Deca Homes brand of 8990 Holdings.

How large is the development?

Official project information describes approximately 13 hectares with 22 residential towers and a 1.3-hectare central park and amenity area.

What unit sizes are available?

The main layouts are 2BR B at 30.60 sqm, 2BR A and 2BR C at 35.57 sqm, and a 3BR option at 42.07 sqm.

Are ready-for-occupancy units available?

Ready-for-occupancy options may be available depending on current inventory. Exact unit availability should be confirmed before reservation.

Can I use Pag-IBIG financing?

Pag-IBIG options may be available subject to the unit, borrower eligibility, documentary requirements and financing approval.

How much is the monthly amortization?

It depends on your selected unit, loan amount, financing type, interest rate and loan term. Request the latest approved computation for accurate figures.

What amenities are available?

Official project information lists community features including a clubhouse, basketball courts, parks, playground, jogging path, outdoor gym, atrium and lagoon.

Does each building have elevators?

The official 8990 project information states that each residential building has four elevators.

Can I schedule a site viewing?

Yes. Use the viewing form below or message MAB through Facebook Messenger.

How does the referral program work?

Qualified referral partners may receive ₱25,000 per successful transaction. The commission is released once the referred buyer is approved, subject to validation and applicable program terms.
