Frequently Asked Questions

Common Buyer Questions

Is Urban Deca Homes Ortigas ready for occupancy?

Ready-for-occupancy options are available based on current inventory. Exact availability should be confirmed before reservation.

What unit sizes are available?

Current unit materials include 2BR B at 30.60 sqm, 2BR A at 35.57 sqm, 2BR C at 35.57 sqm, and 3BR A at 42.07 sqm.

Can I use Pag-IBIG financing?

Pag-IBIG financing options may be available subject to unit eligibility, buyer qualification, approval, and applicable requirements.

How much is the monthly amortization?

It depends on the unit, financing type, loan amount, term, interest rate, and latest approved computation.

How much is the downpayment?

Downpayment and promotional offers may differ by unit, building, and current promotion. Request the latest computation.

Can I schedule a site viewing?

Yes. Message MAB with your preferred date and time.

How does the referral program work?

Qualified referral partners may receive ₱25,000 per successful transaction. Commission is released once the referred buyer is approved, subject to validation and applicable referral terms.
