Project Background

About Urban Deca Homes Ortigas

Urban Deca Homes Ortigas is a large residential community located along Ortigas Avenue Extension in Rosario, Pasig City.

Based on the project reference materials, the development covers approximately 13 hectares and is composed of 22 buildings, with approximately 19,046 residential units.

The community also provides approximately 1.3 hectares of amenity and recreational space, giving residents access to open areas and facilities for exercise, recreation and family activities.

For buyers, the decision should not be based on price alone. The layout, location, financing, community environment and your actual needs should make sense together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers usually ask

What unit types are shown on this website?

The layouts presented here are 2 Bedroom B at approximately 30.60 sqm, 2 Bedroom A at approximately 35.57 sqm, 2 Bedroom C at approximately 35.57 sqm and 3 Bedroom A at approximately 42.07 sqm.

What is the difference between 2BR A and 2BR C?

Both have approximately 35.57 sqm of floor area. 2BR A is shown as an outer-unit configuration, while 2BR C is shown as an inner-unit configuration.

Which unit is the most compact?

2 Bedroom B is the most compact layout shown on this website at approximately 30.60 sqm.

Is there a 3-bedroom option?

Yes. 3 Bedroom A is approximately 42.07 sqm and is shown as an outer-corner configuration.

Can I use Pag-IBIG financing?

Pag-IBIG financing options may be available depending on the unit, buyer eligibility, documentary requirements and financing approval. Ask MAB for the latest applicable process.

How much is the monthly amortization?

The monthly amount depends on the selected unit, approved loan amount, interest rate, loan term and financing institution. The calculator on this website is only for planning.

How much is the downpayment?

Downpayment amounts and promotions can differ depending on the unit, building, financing arrangement and current offers. Request the latest computation before deciding.

What amenities are available?

The project materials show amenities and community spaces including a playground, outdoor gym, basketball court, lagoon, pocket parks, multi-purpose facilities and commercial conveniences.

Where is Urban Deca Homes Ortigas located?

The development is located along Ortigas Avenue Extension in Rosario, Pasig City.

Can I request a site viewing?

Yes. Use the viewing request form below or contact MAB through Facebook Messenger. Your preferred schedule still needs to be confirmed based on viewing availability.

How does the referral program work?

Qualified referral partners may receive ₱25,000 per successful transaction. The referral commission is released once the referred buyer is approved, subject to referral validation and applicable program terms.
